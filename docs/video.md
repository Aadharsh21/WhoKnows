# 90 second video: shot list and script

Record the site at 1440 by 900 in Chrome with the speed control at 1x, and Discord in a second window. Read the voiceover at a relaxed pace; it's about 210 words, which fits 90 seconds with room to breathe. Captions in quotes are what's on screen.

| Time | Screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | The top of the site. Slowly scroll to the player. | It's 1:07 AM. The capstone demo is at nine, checkout is broken, and Nina has no idea who wrote the payments code. |
| 0:07 | Press play. Pulses fly from Nina's window to all nine others. | So her agent asks all nine teammates' agents at once. Each one checks only its own person's work. Two answer. Nobody's phone buzzes. |
| 0:17 | Pause on Nina's phone showing the two quotes. | Omar owns payments, but he's asleep. His agent answers for him, quoting his notes word for word and naming the file. His drafts wait for his OK, and his secrets never come up. |
| 0:29 | Step to 1:35 and 1:38. Show the two change requests waiting on Omar's window. | Nina and Theo both ask to change Omar's file, in the same lines. Neither edits it. Omar's agent drafts both and holds them for him. |
| 0:38 | Step to 7:00. Omar's phone: receipts, the side by side pick, the commit card. | At seven he wakes up to a receipt of what his agent did overnight, picks Nina's fix, and the change goes out like a commit, to the three people it affects, not all ten. |
| 0:50 | The trust question, then 7:50: Nina's one-line change saves itself, with undo. | Then his agent asks: let Nina's small changes through next time? He says yes. Her next one saves on its own, and he can undo it in one tap. |
| 0:59 | The board: "Checkout page: Nina (Ade is backup)". | Every half hour the agents also check that no part of the demo depends on one person. |
| 1:04 | Switch to "If an agent goes rogue". Red stops on the stage. | And if an agent gets tricked into stealing secrets or wiping a file, the gate stops it. It doesn't trust the agent. |
| 1:12 | Scroll to "In numbers". Hold on the table. | We ran the real agents against a week of questions on generated teams of thirty. A group chat notified 29 people per question. WhoKnows notified about one, and its agents never pinged anyone asleep. |
| 1:22 | Discord: Swayam types "who knows the payments code?" and taps Approve on a change. | It runs live in Discord, with buttons for every decision. |
| 1:27 | Back to the top of the site. Show the repo link. | WhoKnows. Your agent asks theirs, so you don't have to. |

## Tips

- Turn on the site's caption bar; it already says most of this, so the voiceover can be calm.
- In Discord, take Nina's seat on one account and Omar's on the other, so the approve button is a real tap by a real person.
- If you run long, cut the 0:59 board shot first. Keep the numbers.
- Export at 1080p. Add the repo URL as a lower third for the last five seconds.
